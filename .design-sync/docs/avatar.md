Circular avatar. Compose: AvatarImage (src, alt) + AvatarFallback (initials shown while loading or on error).

Parts: `Avatar`, `AvatarImage`, `AvatarFallback`

```tsx
<Avatar>
  <AvatarImage src="/images/npc-blackwood.jpg" alt="Lord Blackwood" />
  <AvatarFallback>LB</AvatarFallback>
</Avatar>
```
